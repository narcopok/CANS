About CANS

CANS started as a project built by a computer science student who wanted a single place to watch, play, and jam with friends — without switching between five different apps to do it.

What began as a way to practice web development turned into something bigger: a platform focused on shared experiences. Watch videos together, play games together, and create music together, all in one space.

This project is still growing. Every feature you see is being built, tested, and improved as part of an ongoing learning journey in software development — from front-end design to back-end logic.
